# Brag plan: Change-detection and risk-intelligence platform (Selected work 04)
Source: PDF project 03. Client, detection signals and listing source withheld.
1. What: a platform that sweeps a very large population of public listings each week for a specific status change, confirms changes against independent sources, and turns only confirmed events into qualified sales leads.
2. Who: a company whose business depends on that detection.
3. Strongest claim: Stage 1 never triggers outreach; only a delayed, independently corroborated recheck produces a lead, which keeps false positives away from customers.
4. Key visual: weekly sweep (42k checks) -> presence check -> append-only snapshots -> stage 1 flag (no outreach) -> stage 2 recheck -> corroborate + registries -> discarded or qualified lead (scoring + case board).
5. Verbatim: "Weekly sweep, 42k checks", "Stage 1 flag, no outreach", "Stage 2 recheck", "Corroborate + registries", "Discarded", "Qualified lead".
6. Tone: cinematic surveillance. Edit style: top-down radar sweep, HUD, no drone. Ice cyan #5fe1ff on #061219.
7. 1080x1350, 12 s, silent loop.
## Storyboard
1. 0.0-2.8 Hook: a field of listings, a radar sweep rotates, "WEEKLY SWEEP · 42K CHECKS".
2. 2.8-5.6 A handful of points flare amber: "Stage 1 flag. No outreach."
3. 5.6-8.8 Recheck: most flags fade back to grey ("Discarded"), one is corroborated by lines to registries.
4. 8.8-12 It becomes a qualified lead card on the case board; close "Detection is easy. Not crying wolf is the work." Fade.
