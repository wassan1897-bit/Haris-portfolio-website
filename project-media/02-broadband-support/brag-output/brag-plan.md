# Brag Plan: AI Customer Support for a UK Fibre Broadband Provider

## What is this?
End-to-end AI customer support: a chat agent runs the conversation and calls six stateless, single-job backend workflows (account lookup, coverage check, outage check, prospect creation, ticketing and routing, metrics).

## Angle
Show one real support conversation travelling through the system: the customer writes in, the agent calls only the workflows it needs, the ticket lands with 1 of 7 departments and the customer gets an email. Then the numbers.

## Hook
The chat panel rises and a customer writes "My broadband has been down since this morning." The amber agent node wakes up and reads the intent.

## Punchline
Verified results count up: 6,700+ conversations, 2,400+ tickets across 7 departments, 21,000+ agent minutes saved. A chip notes the same backend was later reused by a phone voice agent.

## Tone and format
Polished, quiet, product-film. Landscape 1920x1080, 12 s, silent, loops (first and last frame are the empty stage).

## Visual identity
Stage ink navy #0d1422 to #19243d (radial). Amber #f5b43b from the client's diagram for the agent and active calls. Dark node cards #17223a, text #eef2f8. White chat panel, navy #14213f customer bubbles. Inter.

## Storyboard
1. A customer writes in (0-2.8s): chat panel, customer message, typing, AI reply; agent node appears with "Intent: no connection".
2. Six single-job workflows (2.8-5.6s): six workflow nodes and lines draw; pulses call Account lookup ("Existing customer") and Outage check ("No outage in the area").
3. Routed to the right team (5.6-8.4s): pulse to Ticketing and routing; 1 of 7 departments lights; customer email sent; Metrics logs it; chat confirms.
4. Verified results (8.4-12s): chat and diagram leave, three counters, reuse chip, hold, fade to empty stage.

Note: the chat wording is illustrative (the client gave no transcript). Workflow names, department count and result figures are the client's.
