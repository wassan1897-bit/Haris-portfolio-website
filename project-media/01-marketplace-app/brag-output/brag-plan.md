# Brag Plan: Two-Sided Marketplace App

## What is this app?
A marketplace that connects homeowners with contractors, taken from a web app to native iOS and Android and shipped to both stores by one engineer on a Windows machine, approved by Apple on the first submission.

## The angle
Show the app doing its job, then show it shipping. A contractor goes live, a homeowner's job happens in a live session, the AI turns the finished job into a written page, and the same build flows down the release pipeline into both stores. The brag is the last beat: Apple said yes the first time, from a pipeline with no Mac in it.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Jordan's home screen rises in and the AVAILABLE toggle flips on with a green glow. One clear, satisfying action; the product is alive from frame one.

## Key moments (the middle)
- An incoming request card drops in (Alex Reviewer, Plumbing, "Kitchen tap drips constantly even when fully closed.") and Respond lights up.
- The live session: chat bubbles arrive one by one ("Got it off. It was completely packed." then the fix), then the job photo.
- The AI write-up builds itself: "Replacing a Failed Water Heater in Edmond", the SEO title and meta lines filling in, a Published badge.

## Outro / punchline
The release pipeline draws itself: Web codebase and Compliance pass feed the Native shell, the line splits to Google Play and to Cloud macOS CI, then App Store. A pulse runs both paths; the App Store box stamps "Approved on first submission". Hold, then fade to the opening frame so the loop is seamless.

## User flow worth showing
Contractor switches to available, then gets a request -> live session with a homeowner (messages and a photo) -> finished job becomes an SEO write-up. Then the shipping story (pipeline to both stores).

## Tone
- Preset: polished (with app-store feature clarity)

- Interpretation: few scenes, smooth slides, generous holds; no voice, no hype copy; the UI and the pipeline carry it.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 - Going live - 3.0s
Jordan's home screen (navy header "Welcome back, Jordan") rises into frame on the dark stage. The AVAILABLE card's toggle flips on with a soft green glow ("You're visible to homeowners nearby"). Stats row 14 Sessions, 1 Active Job, 4.8 Rating.
Sequential/interaction: yes - simulated tap flips the toggle at about 1.0s; an Incoming Request card (Alex Reviewer, Plumbing, "Kitchen tap drips constantly even when fully closed.") slides in at about 1.8s and Respond highlights.
Audio intent: none (silent)
Transition mood: soft slide -> Scene 2

### Scene 2 - Live session - 3.2s
The camera glides to the session screen ("Session with Jordan Pro", Completed, Plumbing, 20 min). Chat bubbles arrive one by one and hold: "Got it off. It was completely packed." then "That was the problem. Rinse it, refit hand-tight, run the tap and check for drips." then the job photo bubble.
Sequential/interaction: yes - three bubbles, about 0.7s apart, the full set holds at least 1.2s.
Transition mood: soft slide -> Scene 3

### Scene 3 - The job becomes a page - 2.8s
The marketing screen: "Replacing a Failed Water Heater in Edmond" with a Published chip; the SEO title, meta description and section headings fill in line by line like the AI is writing them.
Sequential/interaction: yes - lines appear top to bottom, then hold.
Transition mood: clean wipe -> Scene 4

### Scene 4 - Shipped to both stores - 3.0s
The pipeline draws left to right: Compliance pass and Web codebase -> Native shell (inside "Windows box, no Mac") -> Signed bundle -> Google Play, and Cloud macOS CI build -> Auto signing + upload -> App Store. A light pulse travels both branches; the App Store box gains a green check and "Approved on first submission". Hold, then fade to the stage colour that opens Scene 1 for a seamless loop.
Sequential/interaction: yes - nodes light in path order, the stamp lands last and holds about 1.2s.
Transition mood: fade to loop start
