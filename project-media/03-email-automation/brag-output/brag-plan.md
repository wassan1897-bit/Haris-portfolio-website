# Brag Plan: Multi-Inbox Email Automation (project 03)

Angle: follow one client enquiry through the system. It lands in a shared inbox, gets classified into one of eight scoped reasoning paths, its files and CRM ledger are handled and the thread is matched, a reply is drafted from the living rules document, checked by the AI compliance pass and a deterministic gate, and lands as a draft for human review. Then the build in numbers.

Format: 1920x1080, 12 s, silent, loops (empty stage first and last frame). Polished product film.
Palette: page teal #0f2a30 (stage radial from it), accent light cyan #8fd8e6, nodes #133b44, text #eef2f8, white mail panel with navy #14213f. Inter.

1. An enquiry lands (0-2.8s): shared inbox panel, a new document request with two attachments slides in; the AI classify node wakes.
2. Eight scoped paths (2.8-5.4s): eight lanes fan out, one lights; side effects: attachments filed to OneDrive, CRM ledger updated, thread matched to its record.
3. Drafted, checked, gated (5.4-8.4s): draft writer and AI compliance pass both read the living rules doc; deterministic gate clears it to "Draft in mailbox" (the held path to human escalation stays dim); the inbox row shows "Draft ready for review".
4. The build (8.4-12s): 224-node email pipeline, 38-node web-intake pipeline, 8 reasoning paths; chip "Live in production on a real client inbox".

Note: the email sender, subject and file names are illustrative. Node names, counts and claims are the client's.
Audio: intentional silence.
