# Brag plan: Enterprise voice AI agent (Selected work 01)

## Rubric
1. What is it? A Retell AI phone agent for a UK broadband provider. It answers every call, works out who is calling (new, existing, just signed up), collects and confirms the address, checks coverage and live outages through n8n, and hands the caller to the right department with the context kept.
2. Who is it for? A broadband provider's support and sales teams, and their callers.
3. Strongest claim: zero-wait triage with context-preserved handoffs, and it checks outage data before escalating to technical support.
4. Key visual: the Retell conversation-flow canvas (welcome, address collection, service checker, global triage node with department transitions) and the n8n outage-check and AI address-agent workflows.
5. Verbatim copy from the project: "Technical support", "Billing", "Sales", "Renewals", "Retention", "Complaints", "Customer Service" (the transition targets on the global node); "Servicechecker"; "Outage checker".
6. Tone: cinematic (user flag), polished restraint, dark.
7. Format: 4:5 portrait 1080x1350, 60 fps, silent, loops (Selected work preview card).
8. Duration: 12 s (user flag; it is a looping card, not a social post).
9. Privacy: client API URL, brand name and Windows watermark blurred or cropped in src/.

## Angle
The phone rings at night and nobody is waiting: the agent picks up, triages, checks the network and routes. Show the real flow, then land on where every call ends up.

## Storyboard (12 s)
1. Hook, 0.0 to 2.3 s: dark stage, a live call waveform pulsing; mono "INCOMING CALL · 02:14"; Bodoni line "Answered in zero seconds."
2. The flow, 2.3 to 5.4 s: the whole Retell conversation canvas drifts and pushes in; caption "One conversation flow in Retell AI".
3. Triage, 5.4 to 8.3 s: push into the zoomed flow (welcome, address, service checker); caption "Who is calling, where, and is the area covered".
4. Outage check, 8.3 to 10.5 s: the n8n outage-check workflow pans right to left; caption "n8n checks live outages before any escalation".
5. Routed, 10.5 to 12.0 s: the seven departments stack in as chips; Bodoni "Every call, routed."; fade to black for the loop.

No music or SFX (flags).
