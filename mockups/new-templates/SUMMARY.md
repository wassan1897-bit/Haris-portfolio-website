# New website: four template options

Each PNG is one complete homepage, top to bottom, at 1440px wide. The HTML that produced each one is in `src/`.

Inspiration taken from the references (not copied):
- **schemasauce.com**: plain-spoken consultant positioning, portrait with a personal intro, a client-quote band, "what you get", one closing call to action.
- **galenpyle.com**: dark, proof-led layout, "who I work with" by audience, services, and a booking calendar at the end.

All copy uses Haris's real material from the current site: his title, how he works, what he builds, Upwork record (Top Rated Plus, 100% Job Success, 33 jobs, 728 hours, $30K+), project figures and real client quotes. Grey boxes marked "Portrait" or "Photo" are where new photography goes, because the current photos are being removed.

## 01-clarity.png: "Clarity" (light, trust-first)
Cool off-white, near-black text, one emerald accent. Font: Geist. Closest in spirit to Schema Sauce: a split hero with a portrait, a tool-logo strip, a personal intro, an emerald client-quote band, four headline numbers, a "What I build" grid, selected work as clean rows, a four-step "How a project runs", reviews and a dark closing call to action. Best if the goal is calm credibility for business owners.

## 02-operator.png: "Operator" (dark, proof-led)
Graphite background with a signal-lime accent, no glow effects. Fonts: Space Grotesk and DM Sans. Closest in spirit to Galen Pyle: a large hero with a cut-out portrait and floating proof chips, an Upwork record strip, "Who I work with" as an open accordion, a bento of four live systems, a scrolling tool strip, a review carousel and a booking calendar. Best if the goal is a bold, technical first impression.

## 03-ledger.png: "Ledger" (editorial, type-led)
Near-white and near-black with a cobalt accent used sparingly. Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque and Inter Tight. A big statement headline instead of a classic hero, the work as an "Index of work" table where one row expands with a visual and results, oversized numbers, a "How I work" section with four working rules, two large quotes and a contact block with the email and a short form. Best if the goal is to feel like a senior independent, not an agency.

## 04-workbench.png: "Workbench" (shows how the systems work)
Cool bone background, deep forest-green panels, one amber accent. Fonts: Sora and Figtree. The hero shows a diagram of a real pipeline (the white-label estimation tool: photo in, usability check, vision model and fixed rubric, price by SMS and email, quote lines into the CRM). Then a "What I build" bento, work in production as tabs, "What you get / What you will not get", a featured review with an amber Upwork stats card, an FAQ and a green closing panel. Best if the goal is to show clients exactly how the work is built.

Nothing on the live site has been deleted or rebuilt. Choose a template (or mix parts) and the build starts from there.
