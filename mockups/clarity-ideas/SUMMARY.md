# Clarity ideas: five light directions

You liked **Clarity** and want nothing dark, so every option here is light: Clarity's off-white canvas, near-black text and one emerald accent, with no black sections. Each PNG is one full homepage, top to bottom, 1440px wide. The HTML behind each one is in `src/`.

All copy is Haris's real material (title, how he works, Upwork record, project figures, client quotes). Grey boxes marked "Portrait", "Photo" or "Screenshot" are where new photography and screenshots go.

Skills used: ui-ux-pro-max (page patterns, contrast checks), design-taste-frontend (anti-cliché rules), high-end-visual-design (framed cards, buttons with the arrow inside), minimalist-ui (Editorial), gpt-taste (Bento), apple-design (Soft).

## 01-clarity-refined.png: Clarity, without the dark parts
The original Clarity you liked, with the black "AI agents" card and the black closing panel turned light (white card with a soft emerald outline; pale mint closing panel), and emerald buttons instead of black ones. Same order: split hero with portrait, tool logos, the four numbers, intro, emerald quote band, what I build, selected work, how a project runs, reviews, closing panel.

## 02-clarity-editorial.png: Clarity Editorial (minimalist-ui)
Calm and document-like. A centred serif headline ("I build the AI your team keeps meaning to get to"), a browser-window frame for a real workflow screenshot, an about block, a bento with hairline borders and soft pastel tags (green, blue, yellow, red) for each kind of work, selected work as clean rows, one large italic quote, an FAQ, and a closing line with a "press B to book a call" shortcut.

## 03-clarity-bento.png: Clarity Bento (gpt-taste)
More energy, still light. A floating pill nav, a split hero with a small portrait pill set inside the headline and an arched portrait on the right, a moving strip of tools, a bento grid (6,700+ conversations as the hero tile), case studies as slices that open sideways, overlapping client initials next to a large quote, and a very large closing line.

## 04-clarity-soft.png: Clarity Soft (apple-design)
Apple-like restraint. A centred headline, then a wide portrait set in a framed panel, four proof cards, "Built around how your team actually works" with a segmented control (AI agents, Automation, Products, Mobile apps), case cards that scroll sideways, three reviews, and a contact card with a proper form.

## 05-clarity-story.png: Clarity Story (ui-ux-pro-max storytelling pattern)
The page reads as a short story, with a chapter bar in the nav (The problem, The approach, The proof, Let's talk). The hook ("Your team is doing work a system should be doing"), then where the week goes (four kinds of busywork and the fix for each), the approach as a four-step path, the proof (numbers, one featured case, three more), and the closing ask. Each chapter has a slightly different light tint and ends with a small "next" link.

Nothing on the live site has been changed. Pick one (or mix parts) and the build starts from there.
