# Four ideas in the schemasauce.com vibe

You asked for this type of website, so all four keep what makes Schema Sauce feel the way it does:
- the deep navy gradient and the fresh green gradient on white;
- one clean sans-serif throughout, with modest headline sizes and roomy 18 to 19px body text;
- the short green dashed line under headings and a rounded portrait card with a personal intro;
- a green band for a client quote and a navy band with the pixel-staircase texture;
- plain-spoken copy ("No hype. No black boxes.") and a green call-to-action band;
- a footer with green column headings.

Nothing is copied: the copy, structure details and logo are Haris's own. Each PNG is a full homepage, 1440px wide, and the HTML is in `src/`.

All copy is Haris's real material: his title, how he works, Upwork record (Top Rated Plus, 100% Job Success, 33 jobs, 728 hours, $30K+), project figures checked against each project, and real client quotes. Grey boxes are where his photos go.

**One readability fix applied to all four.** On Schema Sauce, white text sits on the light lime end of the green band and is hard to read. Here, the green bands and buttons are a shade deeper, and small green text uses a darker green, so everything passes the 4.5:1 contrast check (3:1 for large text).
