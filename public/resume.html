AI GTM Engineer

Working directly with founders and revenue leaders

I build the AI systems behind every stage of the revenue engine: signal detection, account research and enrichment, outbound, inbound conversion and retention. I understand what the number has to do, and I build the machine that does it, to a standard where you can let it run.

The systems revenue teams run on

  • A signal engine that watches 38,548 businesses and tells sales the moment one of them needs help. 51 qualified leads in its first four days.

  • Revenue agents that captured 731 prospects for a national broadband provider while handling more than 8,700 customer conversations.
